# Healthcare B2B Payment Infrastructure Is Rebuilding

**How does the payment stack evolve as we move beyond the low hanging fruit of RCM automation with AI?**
## Overview
We break down the payment infrastructure stack into 4 main layers. We anchor with payment integrity, claims infrastructure, RCM automation, and payer intelligence as the key investment categories.
The \$150B payment infrastructure between payers and providers processes \~\$4T in annual claims across 9B+ transactions, but it was built for paper checks, COBOL batch processing, and 80-character ACH limitations. Three separate markets -- RCM automation, payment integrity, and payer policy intelligence -- are consolidating into a single platform opportunity as AI makes real-time claims adjudication possible.
## What This Means Going Forward
1. **RCM automation** is the entry wedge but commoditizing fast. The real value is in the data generated from processing millions of claims.
2. **Payment integrity** (\$8-10B market) is ripe for AI disruption. Current solutions are rules-based and retrospective. AI enables prospective, real-time fraud and waste detection.
3. **Claims infrastructure** -- the pipes connecting payers and providers -- is the most defensible layer. Whoever controls the real-time claims adjudication layer captures the most durable value.
4. **Payer intelligence** -- understanding payer policies, authorization requirements, and reimbursement rules at scale -- is an emerging category that AI uniquely enables.
The convergence thesis: companies that start in one layer and expand across the stack will build the most valuable businesses. The data flywheel from claims processing feeds payment integrity feeds payer intelligence.
